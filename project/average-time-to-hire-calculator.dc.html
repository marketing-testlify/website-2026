How it works.

Time to hire, explained

Time to hire measures the days from a candidate entering your pipeline to accepting an offer, averaged across roles. The industry sits around 36 days — screening on skill early is one of the fastest ways to cut it.

Why it matters.

Why use an average time to hire calculator?

  • Improved efficiency: spot bottlenecks in the hiring process and streamline recruitment stages.
  • Better candidate experience: a shorter time-to-hire means transparent communication and swift decisions.
  • Data-driven decisions: compare hiring times across roles and adjust strategy accordingly.
  • Resource allocation: know which roles take longer to fill and plan support accordingly.
  • Talent retention: a lengthy process risks losing candidates to other offers — track and shorten it.
