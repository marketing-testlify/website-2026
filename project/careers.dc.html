How we hire.

The interview/hiring process

At Testlify, we follow a comprehensive and fair hiring process to ensure that we select the best candidates for our team. Here's an overview of our interview process:

1

Application review & screening

Once you submit your application, our hiring team will review your resume and assess your qualifications and experience.

2

Technical assessment

Depending on the position, we may ask you to complete a technical assessment to evaluate your knowledge and problem-solving abilities related to software testing.

3

In-person/remote interviews

If you pass the technical assessment, we'll invite you for in-person or remote interviews with our hiring team. These interviews will focus on assessing your skills, cultural fit, and ability to contribute to our team.
