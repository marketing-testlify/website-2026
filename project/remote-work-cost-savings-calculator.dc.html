Why it matters.

Why use a remote work cost savings calculator?

  • Understand financial impact: get a clearer picture of savings from cutting office space and commuting expenses.
  • Budgeting and planning: adjust budgets or allocate resources more efficiently.
  • Cost reduction: evaluate savings potential to make informed decisions about office space.
  • Employee preferences: decide how many remote days to offer based on company goals.
  • Support sustainability: calculate reductions in commuting emissions and office energy use.
  • Enhance satisfaction: reinvest savings into remote tools, wellness programs or incentives.
